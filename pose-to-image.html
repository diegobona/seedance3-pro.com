Explore the controls ↗
01 / BODY

13-point pose control

Move the head, chest, pelvis, shoulders, elbows, hands, knees, and feet. Ragdoll inverse kinematics (IK) lets connected body parts follow as you drag.

02 / CAMERA

Find your framing

Drag empty space to orbit the scene and scroll to zoom. The captured camera view becomes part of the reference you send to image generation.

03 / REFERENCE

Capture a clean reference

Send the visible mannequin and camera view to the image workspace. Editing handles stay out of the capture, leaving a clear reference for your prompt.

04 / ITERATION

Start, adjust, undo

Try crossed arms, kneeling, or jogging presets in the editor. Refine them with the handles, use undo and redo, or reset the selected mannequin.

A useful starting point

Guide the pose.
Review the result.

A captured mannequin scene is a visual reference. The image model interprets it alongside your prompt, so a generated image may differ in anatomy, pose, camera angle, or placement.

Start with a readable silhouette and clear limb placement. Check hands, overlapping limbs, and body proportions in every result. Exact pose matching and identity preservation are not guaranteed.

Pose to Image FAQ

Before your
first pose.

What does this AI pose generator do?

Seedance combines a manual 3D pose editor with reference-guided image generation. You pose the mannequin and choose the camera view, then attach a capture in the image workspace and describe the image you want. The image model interprets that reference when generating.

Can I use the pose editor without generating an image?

Yes. You can open the editor, adjust the mannequin and camera, and prepare a reference without submitting a generation request. Image generation is a separate action that requires sign-in and sufficient credits.

Can I pose multiple characters at once?

The current online editor works with one mannequin. You can control its pose and camera view, then use that capture as a reference for a character image.

Will the image match the pose exactly?

Exact matching is not guaranteed. The reference guides body pose and camera framing, but the model can reinterpret anatomy, overlaps, and placement. The mannequin also does not establish a person's identity. Refine the reference and prompt if the output misses your intention.

Does the editor export OpenPose data?

No. This workflow captures a rendered image of the 3D scene. It does not provide an OpenPose skeleton, keypoint file, or ControlNet pose map export.

Can I turn the pose directly into a video?

The current Pose to Image workflow sends your reference to an image model for a still image. To continue into motion, use the resulting image in a compatible external image-to-video tool. This editor does not automatically transfer a pose or animation to video.
