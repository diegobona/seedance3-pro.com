What it is

One context for complex creative direction.

Explore text-to-video and image-to-video generation in the current workspace.

Seedance 2.5 can interpret mixed text, image, video, and audio references and generate video with native stereo sound. Official materials position it for advertising, branding, e-commerce, product design, gaming, and other production workflows where instruction following and visual consistency matter.

The model supports outputs from 4 to 15 seconds across common landscape, square, and vertical aspect ratios. Its context-aware regeneration stage can produce higher-resolution 2K results while revisiting the original creative context instead of relying only on conventional super-resolution.

Seedance 2.5 prompts

Prompt structure that travels well

Write Seedance 2.5 prompts in six parts: subject, action, environment, camera, timing, and sound. Reference materials should each have a clear job rather than being added without explanation.

Product-film example

A brushed aluminum espresso machine on a dark stone counter. Begin with a macro shot of condensation, then orbit clockwise as warm morning light enters from frame left. Keep the product proportions and logo placement from the reference image. At second four, steam rises and the camera pulls back into a clean hero frame. Native sound: soft room tone, one switch click, then a controlled steam release.

Character-motion example

Use the subject identity and clothing from Image 1. Transfer the restrained shoulder and hand motion from Video 1 without copying its background. Medium tracking shot through a rainy neon street, shallow depth of field, natural foot contact, consistent facial features. Match the pace of Audio 1 and end on a two-second close-up.
