Model positioning

Built for velocity, not unnecessary waiting.

Nano Banana 2 Lite is Google’s cost-efficient option for interactive ideation and high-volume image pipelines.

The official model identifier is gemini-3.1-flash-lite-image. Google positions it as the fastest, most cost-efficient member of the Nano Banana family and recommends it as a replacement for the earlier Gemini 2.5 Flash Image model.

Its most useful role is rapid exploration: thumbnail directions, campaign variants, storyboard frames, e-commerce drafts, UI concept imagery, and other tasks where quick feedback matters more than maximum one-shot detail.
