Frequently Asked Questions

This FAQ focuses on Seedance creator intent keywords: prompt writing, image-to-video conversion, motion transfer, short-form output settings, and beat-synced generation workflows.

How do I write a high-converting Seedance 3.0 prompt for product ads?

Use a three-part prompt: scene intent, visual style, and motion behavior. Example: “Use @image1 as first frame, keep clean studio lighting, apply @video1 camera dolly-in, end on logo lockup.” This structure improves control for product ad outputs.

Can Seedance 3.0 generate image-to-video with referenced camera movement?

Yes. Upload a still image as the visual base and a reference clip for motion. In your prompt, explicitly map the motion source, such as “Use @video1 camera path on @image1 subject.”

What is the best Seedance workflow for TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts?

Set aspect ratio to 9:16, keep duration between 6 to 12 seconds, and write prompts with hook-first composition. Add beat-synced timing when audio drives the edit.

Can Seedance 3.0 extend existing clips without restarting from frame one?

Yes. Use clip extension workflows by feeding the existing output as reference, then describe what should continue next. This preserves continuity while iterating faster.

How do I create beat-synced AI music videos in Seedance 3.0?

Upload the audio track, define pacing in the prompt, and specify transition timing around drops or chorus entries. This gives more consistent rhythm alignment in generated video.
