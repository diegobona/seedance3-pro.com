FAQ

How do I write a high-converting Seedance 3.0 prompt for product ads?

Use a three-part prompt: scene intent, visual style, and motion behavior. Example: “Use @image1 as first frame, keep clean studio lighting, apply @video1 camera dolly-in, end on logo lockup.”

Can Seedance 3.0 generate image-to-video with referenced camera movement?

Yes. Upload a still image as the visual base and a reference clip for motion. In your prompt, map the motion source clearly, such as “Use @video1 camera path on @image1 subject.”

What is the best Seedance workflow for TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts?

Set aspect ratio to 9:16, keep duration between 6 to 12 seconds, and use hook-first prompt structure with beat-synced timing.
