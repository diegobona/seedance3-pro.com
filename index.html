What can I create in the Seedance studio?

Create videos with MiniMax H3, generate or edit images with GPT Image 2, and build editable 3D references in Pose Studio. Explore the Seedance guides for prompting and workflow ideas.

Where can I find prompts and examples?

Browse the creative showcase for video ideas and editable scenes. For original works with the exact generation prompts, visit the MiniMax H3 Prompt Library or GPT Image 2 Prompt Library.

How do I turn a pose into an AI image?

Arrange your characters and props, apply a pose preset, then adjust the joints and camera. Capture the scene and send it to the image workspace with a description of the subjects, setting and style. See the pose reference examples for finished results.
