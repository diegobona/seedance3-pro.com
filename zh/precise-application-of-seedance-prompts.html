场景提示词：

一、古风武侠场景：

Instruction: Use a panoramic shot to display mist-wreathed immortal mountains, paired with a low-angle shot looking up at the majestic sword pavilion. Render in traditional Chinese ink wash style, incorporating details like rust on ancient swords and the dynamic movement of flowing sleeves. Choose a color palette of soft golden sunlight interwoven with cool blue light. Cultural setting: an ancient Taoist immortal's abode.

二、赛博朋克场景

Instruction: Use shot transitions to present neon-lit skyscrapers and narrow alleyways. Use ultra-wide-angle lens + shallow depth of field to highlight cyber architecture. Style: Cyberpunk. Details include cyber-modified retro telephones and the light/shadow effects of holograms. Color palette: dazzling neon light effects and cool-toned blue light. Cultural environment: streets of a futuristic tech metropolis.

三、田园治愈场景

Instruction: Use a medium shot to capture a tranquil lakeside cottage, paired with a high-angle shot looking down on green rural grassland. Style: Watercolor painting style + rural pastoral style. Details focus on dewdrops on green leaves and fruits in a woven basket. Color palette: warm yellow lighting, soft morning light. Cultural environment: a Japanese countryside village.

四、悬疑侦探场景

Instruction: Use obscured shots + low-angle shots to create the atmosphere of a dim, ancient library. Style: Black-and-white film style + retro style. Details highlight the texture of old wooden doors and the mechanical parts of a brass clock. Color palette: chiaroscuro light/shadow effects, silver moonlight. Cultural setting: the surroundings of a 19th-century European detective agency.

五、科幻冒险场景

Instruction: Use fast-moving shots + three-dimensional shots to display sci-fi city streets and polar expedition bases. Style: Sci-fi style + Futurism style. Details include the gear texture of mechanical devices and the light/shadow of a holographic map. Color palette: purple glow, sci-fi light effects from backlighting. Cultural environment: a base on a future space colony planet.

六、浪漫爱情场景

Instruction: Use close-up shots to capture water droplets on rose petals and details of couple's bracelets, paired with foreground-emphasizing shots inside a retro cafe. Style: Romanticism style + watercolor painting style. Color palette: warm orange lighting, golden sunset light/shadow. Cultural environment: an artistic district on the streets of Paris.

七、历史战争场景

Instruction: Use long shots + low-angle shots to present the grandeur of an ancient battlefield, paired with high-contrast shots highlighting the cold gleam of weapons and the shadows of gun smoke. Style: Realistic oil painting style + historical style. Details focus on the rust on ancient iron swords and the texture of armor. Color palette: red glow, shadows under the scorching sun. Cultural environment: the site of a medieval European battlefield.

八、奇幻童话场景

Instruction: Use fantasy style + dynamic art style. Use a panoramic shot to display an ice and snow world under the aurora and a blooming cherry blossom forest, paired with a fisheye lens to create a sense of wonder. Cultural environment: a fairy tale town in a magical kingdom.

九、现代都市职场场景

Style: Modern minimalist style + realistic style. Details focus on the light/shadow on computer screens and subtle textures on documents. Color palette: soft morning light, clear daylight. Cultural environment: the CBD of an international metropolis.

十、民俗文化场景

Instruction: Use symmetrical shots + medium shots to display a traditional Chinese courtyard folk market. Style: Traditional Chinese art style + ethnic style. Cultural environment: a folk festival in an ancient town in southern China.

十一、校园青春场景

Cultural setting: a vibrant high school campus.

运镜提示词：

上帝视角俯拍

Vertical high-altitude overhead shot, slowly pushing down slightly, overlooking the character and the entire scene. Comic/manga god's-eye view, neat composition, light and shadow dividing layers, open and expansive frame, strong narrative sense. Suitable for scene establishment or shots conveying loneliness.

低角度仰拍

Comic/manga powerful shot. Ground-level low-angle shot looking upwards, slightly pushing forward. Wide-angle stretches the character's aura. Top light illuminates the silhouette. Background is simple, lines are sharp. Highlights the character's strength, dominance, and highlight moments.

水平摇镜头

Smooth horizontal lateral panning shot, sweeping from left to right across the scene and characters. Medium to full shot composition, even soft light. The frame is coherent and fluid, with a Japanese fresh comic/manga texture. Suitable for daily life, walking, or environment showcase segments.

缓慢推进特写

Japanese anime/manga style. Slowly pushes forward from a full shot of the character's upper body, focusing to an eye close-up. Eyelashes are clear, pupils have highlights. Soft side light illuminates the face. Shallow depth of field blurs the background. Lines are clean and sharp. Emotionally delicate and immersive, with movie-level shot fluidity.

Extreme Speed Impact Push-In. Shonen manga panel. Extremely fast push-in from a long shot, rushing straight towards the protagonist's face. The frame has slight motion blur, strong contrast lighting, emphasizing the intensity in the eyes. Wide-angle lens enhances the sense of impact. Lines are sharp, dynamic and explosive. Suitable for battle/awakening iconic moments.

缓慢拉远镜头

Healing comic/manga texture. Slowly pulls back from a close-up of the character's face, gradually revealing the full scene. Warm-toned soft light, layers of the frame progress gradually. Background blur transitions naturally. Shot movement is steady and soothing, creating a gentle, healing narrative atmosphere.

跟随 / 跟拍镜头

Closely follows behind/to the side of the character, moving in sync with their steps. Slight shake enhances realism. Medium shot, background has dynamic blur. Comic/manga realistic feel. Suitable for walking, running, or chase scenes.

垂直升降镜头

Slowly rises/falls from the character's feet up to a facial close-up. Linear, fluid camera movement. Light gradually illuminates the whole body, showcasing the character's appearance and demeanor. Anime-style precise lines. Suitable for entrance or costume change shots.

甩镜头转场

Quick whip pan to switch between two scenes. The shot has motion blur and speed lines. Comic/manga panel transition, seamless connection, strong dynamism, vivid color contrast. Suitable for time/space shifts, plot jumps, or battle transitions.

45° 斜向移动镜头

Diagonal 45° dolly shot, showcasing scene depth and compositional tension. Comic/manga cinematic feel. Warm/cool color clash lighting. Lines are designed, breaking conventional composition. Suitable for suspense, shonen, or atmospheric segments.

焦点转换镜头

Lens focus slowly shifts from a blurred background to a sharp focus on the character, while slightly pushing forward. Comic/manga delicate camera work, highlighting the subject, focusing emotion. Soft lighting. Suitable for flashback, epiphany, or heart-fluttering moments.

定格后缓慢推进

The frame first freezes on the character's key expression, then slowly pushes in slightly to magnify. Comic/manga panel texture, clear lines, highlights emphasize the eyes. Extremely slow camera movement intensifies emotional tension. Suitable for iconic moment freezes or emotional outburst shots.
