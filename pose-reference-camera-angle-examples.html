You know the picture you want. One person is sitting, another is standing behind them, and the dog belongs at the front—not hidden behind a chair. Explaining all of that in a prompt can take longer than simply showing it.

Pose Studio lets you arrange people, animals and everyday props in one 3D scene. Pick a pose, move things into place, choose your view, and use that picture as your AI image reference.

From an idea to a reference in a few minutes

  1. Add your cast. Choose the people and animals you need. Add another character whenever the scene needs one.
  2. Start with presets. Pick sitting, walking, a gesture or an action pose, then adjust the body if you want something more specific.
  3. Give them a place. Add furniture or other props. Move, rotate and resize each object until the arrangement reads clearly.
  4. Find your view. Orbit around the scene, use a camera shortcut, or pan with Shift + left-drag. Choose colors that make the shapes easy to distinguish.
  5. Make the image. Click Pose to Image and describe the people, setting and style. Or download the reference and use it in another image tool.

What should you put in the prompt?

Let the reference show the arrangement. Use words for the details it cannot show: who the people are, their clothes, the place, the time of day and the mood.

Keep the people, animals, poses and furniture in the positions shown. Turn this scene into a warm garden photograph. Dress the people in relaxed summer clothes, add natural fur to the pets, and use soft afternoon light. Keep everyone fully visible.

For a busy scene, also state how many people and animals you want. Give each one a simple description, such as “the seated person on the left” or “the cat on the stool.”

A few small choices make a big difference

  • Leave a gap between subjects when their arms, legs or tails overlap too much.
  • Check the view from the camera you will actually use. A good pose can disappear behind a table.
  • Use body and background colors to make your reference easy to read.
  • After generation, check the details that matter to you—especially hands, pet count, furniture and the crop. A reference guides the image; it does not lock every detail.
About these examples

The nine new reference-to-image examples use Pose Studio’s current character models, pose presets and props, arranged for this article. The references were rendered from saved scene setups and sent to the site’s image service using developer API credits. Each new example shows its first generated image, without retouching. Ten requests were submitted; one timed out and is not shown as a finished example. The earlier six examples use captures from the previous single-person editor.

Results vary with the image model and prompt. These examples show possibilities, not a guarantee of exact poses or object placement.
