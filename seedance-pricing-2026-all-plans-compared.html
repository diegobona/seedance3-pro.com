Seedance Pricing 2026: All Plans Compared

Seedance is ByteDance's AI video generator, and its pricing is more fragmented than most tools — because access runs through multiple platforms, each with its own credit system and cost structure. This guide cuts through the noise and tells you exactly what you'll pay and which option makes sense for your workflow.

The Short Answer

You can use Seedance today for free. If you need more output, paid options start at roughly $9.60/month through Dreamina (ByteDance's international platform) — about 20 times cheaper than Sora 2 Pro. For developers, the BytePlus API charges per second of video generated.

Free Tiers: What You Actually Get

Dreamina is the most accessible free option for international users. You get daily credits that reset every 24 hours — enough for roughly 2–3 standard video clips per day. No credit card required, no time limit. The catch: accessing Seedance 2.0 specifically requires a 1 RMB (~$0.14) trial unlock, so it's not entirely frictionless.

Little Skylark (ByteDance's Chinese-market app) is the most generous free tier — 1,200 credits on signup plus 120 daily credits, which works out to about 2 short videos per day indefinitely. The barrier: it requires a Chinese (+86) phone number and the interface is Chinese-only.

Doubao offers 10 free generations per day but requires Feishu beta access and is also Chinese-only.

BytePlus API gives developers 2 million free tokens on signup — enough to prototype a full application before spending anything.

What Actually Drives Your Cost

The credit cost per video isn't fixed — it depends on four variables that stack on top of each other.

Duration is the most obvious multiplier. A 6-second clip costs roughly half what a 12-second clip does, so always start short when testing a new prompt or motion style.

Resolution is where costs jump unexpectedly. Moving from 720p to 1080p roughly doubles credit consumption. Moving to 2K can cost 2–3x the standard rate. Reserve high resolution for final exports, not drafts.

Audio generation doubles the credit cost per video. If you're adding music or voiceover in post-production anyway, disable audio generation entirely — it's the easiest cost to cut.

Retries are the silent budget killer. Each failed or unsatisfactory generation burns full credits. A practical rule: cap yourself at two draft attempts and one final pass. If you're still not happy, the problem is usually the prompt, not the model.

Dreamina vs. Third-Party Platforms

The official Dreamina plans are significantly cheaper, but international users sometimes run into friction — Chinese payment methods (Alipay, WeChat Pay) may be required for certain features, and the 1 RMB trial unlock can be awkward without a Chinese payment method.

Third-party platforms like seedance2.app charge roughly 4x more than Dreamina Basic, but they offer English interfaces, standard international payment processing, and no dependency on Chinese platform accounts. For individuals, that premium is hard to justify. For teams where employee time is expensive, it often makes sense.

How Seedance Compares to Competitors

Seedance wins on price at every tier and is the only major model offering a genuine free option with no credit card. The trade-off is platform fragmentation and, for non-Chinese users, some access friction on the cheapest plans.

Which Plan Should You Choose?

Just testing:​ Start with Dreamina's free tier. Two to three clips per day is enough to evaluate whether Seedance fits your workflow.

Regular creator (under 20 videos/month):​ Dreamina Basic at ~$9.60/month. Best value available, period.

Heavy creator (20+ videos/month or consistent 1080p output):​ Dreamina Pro at ~$45/month. The lower per-video cost pays off quickly at this volume.

Developer building an app:​ BytePlus API. Start with the 2M free tokens, then move to the Lite tier ($0.010/sec) for cost-efficient scale.

Team or agency that wants zero Chinese platform dependency:​ Third-party platforms starting at $41/month. You're paying for convenience and reliability, not raw generation capacity.
