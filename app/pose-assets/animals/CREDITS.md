## Dog

The Pose Studio dog model is adapted from **"Dog Low Poly (Rigged)" (Jack Russell dog) by crownjoshua**.

- Original work and license notice: <https://opengameart.org/content/dog-low-poly-rigged>
- License: [CC0 1.0 Universal public-domain dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- Source OBJ archive: <https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/OBJ.zip>
- Original Blender source: <https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Dog%20%28Jack%20russel%29%20%20Release.blend>
- Retrieved: September 23, 2026.

Pose Studio applied two Catmull–Clark subdivision passes, removed the detached cartoon eyes and eyebrows, reshaped the ears, skull, cheeks and upper neck, and added the editor's skeleton, skin weights and materials. Attribution is provided as a courtesy; CC0 does not require it.

## Pose Studio adaptations

Seedance Pose Studio modified the source meshes for this editor: mesh cleanup and simplification/retopology, normalization of size and orientation, new skeletons and skin weights, and material adjustments. The adapted models are not the unmodified original downloads. Original authors and source contributors do not endorse Pose Studio.

Preserve the author credits, source links, license link, and modification notice when redistributing these cat and horse models or further adaptations. The original accompanying license notices are retained alongside this file.
