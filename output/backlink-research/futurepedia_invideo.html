How invideo AI fits into your stack

Browser

Invideo AI and its timeline editor run in the browser at ai.invideo.io, where users prompt agents, upload media, edit timelines, and export videos.

Automation and APIs

Invideo provides an MCP server that connects its capabilities to Claude, Claude Desktop, Cursor, and Windsurf, allowing those assistants and tools to invoke invideo functionality.

Team chat and tickets

Invideo supports shared workspaces with multiple seats and invitations, enabling team members to collaborate on projects rather than passing files between users.

Limitations & when to consider alternatives

Known limitations

  • Free-plan restrictions. The Free plan has limited credits and exports may include an invideo watermark.
  • Credits do not roll over. Unused monthly credits expire rather than carrying into the next month.
  • Upload size limit. Uploaded images and videos must not exceed 2GB per file.
  • UGC ad duration. The UGC Ads workflow is designed for videos around one minute or less; longer 5- or 10-minute videos are unsupported.
  • Some language subtitle gaps. Subtitles are not supported for several listed languages, including Hindi, Arabic, Urdu, Hebrew, Thai, and Farsi.

Look elsewhere when

  • You need a native desktop installer
  • You need programmatic API access

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use invideo AI for free?

Yes. The Free plan requires no card and provides limited credits, image generation, and social sharing.

What is a credit, and how is it charged?

Credits are used for video creation, generative models, and other AI features; downloading does not consume credits.

Can I generate videos in other languages?

Yes. Invideo supports more than 50 languages, with 11 listed as fully supported.

How can I upload images and videos?

Upload images, videos, or audio from the Media panel, Media Library, or Add files control. Images and videos can be up to 2GB.

How many team members can I have?

Plus, Max, and Generative are individual plans. Team supports 2 to 30 seats.

Can I download and use AI-generated media?

Yes. Generated media remains accessible after cancellation and can be downloaded from the project.
