Questions answered

Before you publish

When does my AITopTools listing go live?+

You review the generated listing before payment. After Stripe confirms payment, your AITopTools page is published immediately and joins the homepage New AI Tools feed. The $249 package does not wait for external directory work to begin.

Is my listing permanent?+

Yes. The Instant Listing is a one-time payment for a permanent AITopTools listing, with no recurring listing fee.

Can I edit my listing later?+

Yes. After your listing is live, you can manage its details and respond to reviews through the owner tools.

Can I see how many views my listing receives?+

Yes. Your owner dashboard includes daily Performance Views for your AITopTools listing, so you can transparently see how many times that listing was viewed each day.

Does the $249 package guarantee directory acceptance?+

No. The $249 package guarantees submission to 100+ additional directories in the workflow, not acceptance or publication by those independent third parties. Each directory controls its own review, timing, and policies.

When will I receive the 100+ directory completion report?+

Your AITopTools page goes live immediately after payment. The external submissions are completed as a separate, human-reviewed process, and the completion report is sent when that work is finished with available links and proof.

What kinds of AI tools can be listed?+

We welcome legitimate AI products, apps, agents, APIs, and AI-powered services with a public website and clear product information. Listings must follow our editorial, safety, and acceptable-use policies.
