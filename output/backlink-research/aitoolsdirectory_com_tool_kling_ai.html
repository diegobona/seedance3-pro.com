    Kling AI

    Kling AI is an AI video and image generator from Kuaishou, the Chinese short-video company. You describe a shot or upload an image, and Kling turns it into a video clip, now with synchronized sound and up to native 4K resolution. The current model is Kling 4.0.

    What sets Kling apart from most generators is how much control it gives you. Many AI video tools work like a slot machine: you pull the handle and hope. Kling gives you levers instead, such as keyframes and reference images that keep characters consistent from shot to shot. That's why it shows up in professional work, from Cannes Lions-winning ads to the Amazon Prime series House of David.

    Kling 4.0 and the video models

    Kling 4.0 handles fast, dynamic motion and can generate a 30-second clip in one go, instead of stitching short pieces together. Omni reference keeps the same character or product consistent across shots, and multiple keyframes let you set the frames a scene has to pass through.

    Kling 3.0 and 3.0 Omni are still available, with synchronized audio and video plus intelligent storyboarding. Motion Control recreates complex movement and facial expressions from a reference video using motion capture, so you can film a rough performance yourself and have a generated character act it out. Native 4K video is aimed at commercial production, where 1080p upscaled footage doesn't hold up.

    Images, storyboards and Canvas

    Kling Image 3.0 generates stills with a strong sense of cinematic framing, at up to native 4K. It can also produce a series of images in a consistent style, which works well for storyboards. You can give it a frame and ask it to predict the next scenes, for example.

    Canvas is Kling's workspace for longer projects. You brainstorm and write scripts with an AI agent, generate images and video on a node-based board, and then refine the result through several rounds of edits without losing the context of the project. Skills handle specific jobs, such as posters or e-commerce product assets, and several can run at once.

    API, MCP and the CLI

    Developers can use Kling through its API, and Kling also offers MCP and a command-line tool so AI agents can drive it. That's useful for batch work: generating many versions of an ad to test styles, or automating a production pipeline. There are also mobile apps for iOS and Android.

    Plans and credits

    Kling runs on credits, and every generation uses some. The cost depends on the model and the length of the clip, so a 30-second 4K clip with audio uses far more than a short 1080p test. Check the credit cost before you generate, especially while you're still refining a prompt.

    Business plans are also available. Monthly subscription credits expire after 31 days, while credits you buy separately stay valid for two years. When a subscription ends, its unused monthly credits are lost.

    Is Kling AI free?

    Yes, with limits. Non-subscribers get a monthly allowance of free credits, and Kling runs occasional promotions with extra credits. Free videos carry a watermark and can't be used commercially, and the 1080p and 4K options are for subscribers.

    Can I use Kling AI videos commercially?

    Only on a paid subscription. Paid subscribers can use and modify generated content commercially, as long as it isn't used to build a product that competes with Kling. You're still responsible for checking rights to anyone's likeness, and any local rules on labeling AI-generated content.
