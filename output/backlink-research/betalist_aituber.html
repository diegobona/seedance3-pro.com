AITuber

Create faceless AI videos automatically for YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram

AITuber is an AI video generator for faceless videos and marketing content. It creates videos for YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram from an idea, script, blog post, or song. Choose from templates like faceless shorts, AI music videos, skeleton videos, 3D talking characters, UGC reaction hooks, product demos, and avatar videos. AITuber writes the voiceover, selects visuals, adds music, syncs captions, and cuts scenes together automatically, so no editing skills are needed. Creators can grow faceless channels, while marketers and agencies can produce UGC-style ads, explainers, and social content at scale.
