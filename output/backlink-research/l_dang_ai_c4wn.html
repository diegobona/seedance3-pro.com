Seedance 3.0 Features

Two core capabilities: multi-material input and precise editing.

01

Drop in images, video clips, and audio together. Seedance 3.0 gives each file its own job, such as the subject, the camera move, or the beat, and turns them into one finished video.

  • Mix images, video, and audio in a single prompt
  • People and products stay consistent across shots
  • Audio sets the rhythm and the voice-over

Example: 2 images + 1 video + 1 audio track → an 8-second first-person fruit tea ad

02

Point to one thing in your video, like a product, an outfit, a person, or the background. Seedance 3.0 changes only that and keeps the motion, light, and camera work as they were.

  • Swap products, people, or outfits
  • Original motion and camera path stay the same
  • No need to regenerate the whole clip

Example: 1 source video + 1 packaging image → every bag in the clip replaced

What can you do on Seedance 3.0?

01

Write the way you talk

Describe your scene in Seedance 3.0: who is there, where they are, and what they do. Try “a kitten chasing a butterfly in a garden.” Add the weather, colors, or a note like “move the camera closer” to make the scene more specific.

Text to video
02

Make a photo move

Upload a clear picture to Seedance 3.0 and describe the movement you want. Make a drawn character wave, or move the camera toward a bunch of flowers. The picture gives AI a starting point. Your words describe what happens next.

Image to video
03

Add sound to the scene

Want footsteps, rain, or someone speaking? Choose a model that supports sound and include it in your description. Start with a simple scene, then listen to the result to check that the sound fits the action and mood.

04

Try a different look

Try the same story in Seedance 3.0 as a cartoon, a clay animation, or a movie scene. Describe the look you want, or add an example picture in a mode that supports image references. Compare a few versions to find the right feel.

Change a detail and try again

Is the background too dark, or the action not quite right? Change your description and make another version. To work on an existing clip, choose a model with video editing, upload the video, and explain what you want to change.

    1. Describe your idea

    Describe your idea in Seedance 3.0: who is there, where they are, and what happens. Add a reference picture if you want a certain character or product. Use a clear image with a subject that is easy to see.

    Step 03
