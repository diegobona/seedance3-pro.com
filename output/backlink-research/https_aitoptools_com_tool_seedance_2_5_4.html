About Seedance 2.5 on Yovid

Yovid runs ByteDance's Seedance 2.5 video model in the browser. Turn a prompt, a pair of frames or up to 40 reference files (30 images and 10 video clips) into one continuous 4–30 second clip, with speech, ambience and effects generated alongside the picture. Draft at 480p, then finish the take you like at 1080p. No watermark, free credits for new users.
