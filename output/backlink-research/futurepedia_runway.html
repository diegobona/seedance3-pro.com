What is Runway?

Runway is designed to revolutionize the landscape of art, entertainment, and human creativity. As an applied AI research company, Runway offers a suite of tools aimed at advancing creativity through artificial intelligence. Whether you are a filmmaker, digital artist, or creative professional, Runway simplifies complex tasks, enabling you to create high-fidelity, controllable video content quickly and efficiently.

Runway stands out with its Gen-3 Alpha video generation tool, which is a game-changer for filmmakers and digital artists. Its ability to produce high-fidelity, controllable video content quickly and efficiently sets it apart in the industry. Additionally, Runway Studios and its various initiatives foster a collaborative and innovative environment, further enhancing its appeal to creative professionals.

Runway excels in providing advanced AI-driven tools that significantly enhance the creative process, making it an essential platform for filmmakers, digital artists, and other creative professionals. Which Runway plan should you pick?

Start with Free to test Runway; upgrade to Standard for regular access to all image and video models, Pro for audio and custom voices, Max for heavy usage, or Enterprise for team controls and SSO.

Free

Good for individuals exploring Runway’s AI tools who need a limited, one-time credit allocation.

Standard

Good for curious creators who need all AI image and video models, 625 monthly credits, and no watermarks.

Pro

Good for creators who need audio tools, custom voices, 2,250 monthly credits, and more storage.

Max

Good for heavy users who need 9,500 monthly credits, credit rollover, highest volume, and studio-grade formats.

Enterprise

Good for teams scaling AI video production who need custom credits, SSO, analytics, teamspaces, and priority support.

How Runway fits into your stack

Browser

Runway's primary workspace is the web app, where teams generate, edit, analyze, and organize creative assets through Tools, Apps, Agent, and Workflows.

Automation and APIs

The Developer Platform exposes authenticated image, video, and audio generation APIs. Workflows can be published as API endpoints, while Runway MCP connects the product to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and Replit.

Team workspaces

Team and Enterprise workspaces support invited members, shared projects and assets, comments, role-based permissions, shared credits, and enterprise SSO.

Mobile

Dedicated iOS and Android apps provide mobile access to Runway generation features, with account work syncing between phone and computer.

Limitations & when to consider alternatives

Known limitations

  • Free credits are one-time. The Free plan includes 125 credits that do not renew or refresh after being spent.
  • Free outputs include watermarks. Videos generated on the Free plan include a Runway watermark.
  • Credit-based generation limits. Standard, Pro, Unlimited, and Max monthly credits reset according to plan rules; additional generations consume credits.
  • SSO is Enterprise-only. SSO is currently available only on Enterprise plans and is unavailable in the Runway iOS app.
  • Prompt and input restrictions vary by model. Models impose limits such as maximum prompt length, supported media types, file sizes, resolutions, and aspect ratios.

Look elsewhere when

  • You need a dedicated coding tool for programming code generation.
  • You need SAML or SCIM rather than OIDC SSO.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Runway have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 forever and includes 125 one-time credits, selected models, and 5GB of asset storage.

What does Runway generate?

Runway supports AI-generated images, videos, audio, editing, and multi-shot creative content through Tools, Apps, Agent, and Workflows.

Can Runway use uploaded media?

Yes. You can upload images, videos, and audio as references or generation inputs.

Does Runway offer an API?

Yes. Runway Dev provides API keys, SDKs, and documented endpoints for programmatic image and video generation.

Does Runway support team workspaces?

Enterprise workspaces support invited members, project membership, roles, permissions, shared credits, and configurable teamspaces.

Does Runway support SSO?

Enterprise workspaces support OIDC-based SSO, including integrations with providers such as Okta and Azure/Entra.
