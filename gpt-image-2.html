Model positioning

Designed for usable creative output.

GPT Image 2.5 emphasizes high-quality generation and editing for tasks that need precise instruction following and flexible image inputs.

OpenAI describes GPT Image 2.5 as its state-of-the-art image generation model for fast, high-quality creation and editing. It accepts text and image inputs, produces images, and supports flexible sizes and high-fidelity input handling.

That combination makes it useful for campaign layouts, product transformations, localized creative, concept frames, readable in-image text, and iterative editing where the original asset still needs to remain recognizable.

Example generation prompt

Design a premium 16:9 launch visual for a compact cinema camera. Black cyclorama studio, narrow white rim light, subtle atmospheric haze, camera centered in the lower third. Add the exact headline “MAKE EVERY FRAME COUNT” in a clean condensed sans-serif above the product. Preserve generous margins and keep all lettering fully legible.

Example editing instruction

Keep the product, logo, camera angle, proportions, and reflections unchanged. Replace only the background with a pale warm-gray paper set, soften the rear shadow, and add a small olive branch on the far right. Do not add any new text.
