Refund policy

A failed generation credit return and a refund of a payment are different processes. Here is how each is handled.

1. Completely unused credit packs2. Failed generations and credit returns3. How to request a payment refund4. Credits during a refund or dispute5. Approved refunds and timing6. Your statutory rights

1. Completely unused credit packs

You can request a full refund within 7 calendar days of purchase if the purchased credit pack is completely unused.

If any purchased credits from that pack have been used, we do not routinely offer a partial refund for the remaining credits. Free daily credits and purchased credits are separate balances; we review the usage attributable to the purchased pack.

Duplicate charges and verified non-delivery are reviewed separately from this unused-pack rule. Please contact us even if the 7-day period has passed when either of those problems applies.

2. Failed generations and credit returns

Confirmed generation failures return credits to their original credit source under the service's generation rules. Daily credits that have already expired are not added to a new day's allowance. For multiple-image requests, charges are based on the images delivered.

A generation credit return changes your credit balance; it does not send money back to your payment method. If a task is stuck or a credit return appears missing, contact support with the task ID and approximate generation time so we can review it.

3. How to request a payment refund

Email zw83716@gmail.com with:

  • The email address on your account.
  • Your order reference or payment receipt reference.
  • The purchase date and the reason for your request.

If you believe there is a duplicate charge or that purchased credits were not delivered, describe the issue and include the relevant order references. Do not send your full card number, card security code, password, or login token.

4. Credits during a refund or dispute

When a refund or payment dispute is recorded, the full credit pack associated with that payment is revoked, including any remaining credits. Purchases and generation are paused pending manual review. This process does not revoke daily free credits, although generation remains paused during the review. Previously used purchased credits can leave a shortfall that needs to be reviewed.

If a refund fails or is canceled, or a dispute is resolved, credit access may still require support review. Contact us rather than assuming that credits or account access will restore automatically.

5. Approved refunds and timing

Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method. They generally take 5–10 business days to appear, depending on Stripe, your bank, and the payment method. Some cases can take longer.

If an approved refund has not appeared after the expected processing period, reply to the support conversation with your order reference so we can check its status.

6. Your statutory rights

This policy does not limit any mandatory consumer rights or remedies available under applicable law.
